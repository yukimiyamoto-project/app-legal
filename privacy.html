Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to iOS apps published by Yuki Miyamoto that link to this page. Features vary between apps, so each section below applies only when an app includes the feature or service described.

Information Processed by the App

Depending on its features, an app may process content you create, app settings, camera or microphone input, selected photo-library items, location information, and purchase or subscription status. This information is used to provide the feature you request.

User-created content and information obtained through Apple permission-protected APIs are generally processed on your device or through Apple services. The developer does not directly receive this information unless you choose to send, share, or submit it, for example in a support email. Data processed by advertising and other third-party services is described separately below.

Camera, Microphone, Photos, and Location

Apps that request these permissions use them only for the relevant app feature, such as capturing a photo or video, recording audio with a video, selecting or saving media, or adding location information. Some apps may embed location information in a photo or video. If you export or share that file, the embedded information may also be provided to the recipient or destination service.

You can change permission choices at any time in the iOS Settings app. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

On-Device Media Processing and Face Data

Some apps allow you to select photos or other media for features such as viewing, editing, or comparison. When an app provides only on-device media processing, a selected image may incidentally contain a face, but the app does not use that image to identify or analyze the person. Unless a face-specific feature and a separate disclosure explicitly state otherwise, these apps do not detect or recognize faces and do not collect, extract, generate, or store face geometry, facial landmarks, face templates, embeddings, identity information, or other biometric Face Data.

In PicVS (also displayed as “画像比較くん” in Japanese), two images selected through Apple's system photo picker are used only on the device to display the images, compare their pixel values, calculate a matching percentage, and create a temporary difference mask. PicVS does not upload selected images or comparison results to developer-controlled servers; does not store them in app-managed files, databases, iCloud, CloudKit, or other cloud storage; and does not provide them to Google AdMob, Amazon, or any other third party. They remain only in volatile app memory for the current comparison and are released from the app's active state when you reset or replace the images, or when the app process ends. Your original photos remain under your control in the Photos library. PicVS therefore collects no Face Data and retains no Face Data for the developer to delete. Portrait images shown in App Store screenshots are demonstration content only and do not represent face-processing functionality.

Storage, iCloud, and Backup

Depending on the app and your Apple settings, app data may be stored locally, included in an iPhone or iPad backup, or synced through Apple services such as iCloud or CloudKit. Local storage, device backup, and CloudKit sync are different services, and not every app uses all of them. Apple processes data used by its services under Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising

Some apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, performance data, and crash or diagnostic information. Google and its advertising partners may use this information to provide ads, measure performance, perform analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable law, personalize advertising.

When required, you can review or change advertising consent choices through “Privacy Options” in the app. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Changing these choices does not necessarily prevent non-personalized advertising or processing required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Amazon Associates Program

Some apps contain Amazon.co.jp affiliate links. When you choose an Amazon link, Amazon receives the request and may process referral information, device or browser information, cookies, account information, and purchase activity under its own privacy policy. The developer may receive aggregated referral and performance reports, but does not receive your Amazon password or payment-card details. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Some apps offer in-app purchases or subscriptions through Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment and account information. The app may receive product, transaction, and entitlement status needed to provide purchased features, but the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Sharing and External Services

Some apps include sharing, collaboration, or links to external services. Information you choose to share may be received by the people and services you select and will be handled according to their terms and privacy policies. Please review the destination and the information being shared before continuing.

Support Communications

If you contact the developer, your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send will be used to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not include sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

App-managed local content is retained until you delete it using the app, remove the app, or otherwise erase the relevant storage. Copies in backups, synced storage, exports, or shared destinations are controlled by the applicable Apple settings or destination service. Information sent directly to the developer is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, comply with law, and resolve disputes.

To withdraw advertising consent, use “Privacy Options” when shown in the app. To change system permissions or tracking choices, use iOS Settings. To request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to the developer, contact the email address below. Third-party services handle their own retention and deletion requests under their privacy policies.
